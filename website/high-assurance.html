The Problem
Evidence that outlives access and trust

High-stakes AI increasingly operates across organizational, contractual, and regulatory boundaries. The parties involved rarely share infrastructure, administrative control, or trust assumptions.

In these environments, conventional artifacts — logs, dashboards, screenshots, and platform attestations — cannot be independently verified, especially when the original operator is unavailable, untrusted, or unable to provide access.

High-assurance environments require evidence that remains verifiable even when the system that produced it is no longer accessible or trusted.

Why Existing Evidence Falls Short
Built for operations, not for independent verification
Most organizations rely on internal logs, platform dashboards, vendor attestations, and ad-hoc reports. While these may serve operational needs, they are rarely sufficient for independent verification.
Logs
Require access to the original platform
Dashboards

In high-assurance environments, these conditions cannot be assumed.

Evidence over trust.

Most AI governance approaches depend on runtime controls, platform assurances, or the continued availability and cooperation of the original operator. High-assurance environments require something stronger: evidence that can be verified offline, independently, and after the fact — without requiring trust in the operator.

Built on NOUS
An evidence layer, not a runtime control
NOUS does not enforce, block, or intervene in runtime behavior. Instead, it generates cryptographic evidence that an execution remained within its declared policy envelope. These artifacts can be verified by any third party, offline, using only standard cryptographic libraries — without access to the original system and without trust in the operator.

What NOUS does not do

  • enforce policy at runtime
  • block or intervene in execution
  • sit in the execution path
  • claim the model cannot misbehave

What NOUS does

  • generate cryptographic evidence of conformance
  • bind the evidence to the declared policy envelope
  • make every artifact offline-verifiable by any third party
  • require zero trust in the operator
Runtime controls and post-execution evidence serve different purposes. NOUS focuses on the evidence layer.

Regulatory Readiness
Evidence that supports compliance — it does not assert it

NOUS supports evidence generation, auditability, and documentation requirements in regulated environments. It produces deterministic, offline-verifiable artifacts that can be used to demonstrate how an AI system behaved within its declared boundaries — aligned with expectations under frameworks such as the EU AI Act.

NOUS does not claim compliance. It provides evidence that can be used to support it.
