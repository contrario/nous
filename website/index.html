How It Works

How NOUS Works

1
Declare the rules
You write the policies your agent must obey. These are not suggestions — they are the law the agent declares for itself.
2
NOUS proves the envelope
NOUS builds a mathematical region that proves the declared rules hold across every path the agent could take. This is the envelope: a formal, all-paths proof of conformance.
3
Runtime conformance is evidenced
When the agent runs, the compliance layer does not guard or block. It records a signed trace that evidences whether the run stayed inside the proven envelope. Evidence, not intervention.
4
Everything is offline-verifiable
The envelope proof, the signatures, the dossier, and the materiality verdict are all re-checkable on a disconnected laptop with only cryptography + stdlib. Zero issuer trust. Not even NOUS.
Why Different

Why NOUS Is Different

The part the academic papers call “an unfulfilled vision”

In formal methods, there’s a clean definition for when a change breaks a system: the new contract must imply the old one. If it doesn’t, the proof is dead.

The theory is elegant. The problem is practical: nobody has a routine way to check that implication in real systems. The research itself admits this — it calls it “a largely unfulfilled vision.”

Most tools fall back to judgment: semver, test suites, “this feels minor.”

NOUS doesn’t.

The contract the papers describe — NOUS already carries it

The coverage proof in NOUS is exactly the contract the literature talks about: a mathematical region that proves the declared rules hold across every path the agent could take.

And when the system changes, NOUS can check whether the new region still contains the old one — offline, deterministically, with Farkas arithmetic, no solver to trust.

This is the part the papers don’t have. NOUS does.

From theory → to evidence that travels

What the research frames as a definition, NOUS turns into: a deterministic sidecar, a sha-pinned artifact, carried inside the Annex IV dossier, re-verifiable offline, bound to the envelope-proof, and routed by the verifier with honest boundaries.

The result is a three-level gradient:

The verifier never pretends to prove the classification. It authenticates it, and points at the leg that does prove preservation.

This is not theory. This is running code.

Why this matters

Because for the first time, “how much did it change” stops being an opinion. It becomes: deterministic, cryptographically bound, offline-verifiable, reproducible, and independent of the vendor.

The academic idea becomes a practical artifact. The proof becomes something that travels. And the system becomes something an auditor can check without trusting anyone — not the vendor, not even NOUS.

Coverage
Over the declared risk threshold, no input satisfies it while escaping every blocking policy. No gap in the net.
HONEST BOUNDARY
Coverage proves there is no gap in the declared blocking net — not that the agent cannot misbehave. Policies are monitors; the signed evidence attests the declared governance was sound and in force. This is coverage, not impossibility. NOUS claims exactly what it can sign, and refuses the rest.
